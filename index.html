Settings

Part types & surface labels

One type per line: Type name | code | A:description; B:description; .... The letters are the checkboxes; the descriptions appear in captions. The diagrams are fixed for codes I, L and C.

Photo size & quality

Photos are saved once, at the stored size/quality, and go into the Word file unchanged when the Word size is the same (no second compression). 1600 px at quality 0.8 is roughly 300-500 KB per photo of masonry (~100 MB for 100 parts). Lower the Word size or generate one area at a time if files get too big for OneDrive.

Quick-pick defects

One per line: Button label | Sentence inserted into the notes. Shown under every surface; the label is what appears in the summary table.
